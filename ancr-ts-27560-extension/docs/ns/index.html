ANCR vocabulary namespaces

Status. The ANCR DPV vocabulary 0.7 is approved by the ANCR Working Group as a Working Group Recommendation and is pending Kantara Leadership Council review. During the pilot these pages are set not to be indexed by search engines. These are not DPV, W3C, ISO/IEC or Council of Europe identifiers. The identifiers are intended to stay the same; definitions may change after Leadership Council review.

The identifiers are intended to be permanent. DPV and other vocabularies link to them; they are not moved into another namespace.
