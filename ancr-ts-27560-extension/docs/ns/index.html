ANCR vocabulary namespaces

Provisional. These identifiers are proposed in the ANCR DPV vocabulary draft 0.7. The ANCR Working Group and Kantara Initiative have not yet accepted them. They are staged by TCIEG for review and will be published at this address only after acceptance. They are not DPV, W3C, ISO/IEC or Council of Europe identifiers. Definitions may change before acceptance; the identifiers are intended to stay the same.

The identifiers are permanent once accepted. DPV and other vocabularies link to them; they are not moved into another namespace.
